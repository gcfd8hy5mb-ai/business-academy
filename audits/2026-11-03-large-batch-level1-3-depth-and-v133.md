# Business Academy — Level 1–3 Depth Audit and Level 3 Core-Lesson Expansion

**Date:** November 3, 2026
**Status:** Fourteen new detailed Level 3 lessons published and source-validated. Comprehensive lesson-depth remediation of Levels 1–3 remains incomplete.

## Source-level depth screens and limitations
The lesson-screening rule flags a lesson when `minutes >= 90` and its body text has fewer than 200 whitespace-delimited words. This is a deliberately narrow triage heuristic: long text does **not** prove strong teaching, and short text may be complemented by real structured reading, applications, quantitative practice and portfolios.

**Level 1:** The first 12 Level 1 scripts were executed together, yielding 67 lessons in that partial assembly, with zero qualifying 90+-minute short-body flags. Additional late Level 1 scripts were not included in that particular count; this is not a final all-source Level 1 inventory.

**Level 2:** A partial early Level 2 assembly (through v55, alongside some Level 1 scripts) showed instructional bodies as brief as roughly 58–71 words in 70-minute lessons. Such lessons fall below the >=90-minute flag threshold but are not thereby cleared of educational-depth concern. The full published Level 2 coursework was not assembled in this depth check.

**Level 3:** The first 11 Level 3 scripts through `level3-independent-transfer-v83.js` generated 137 lessons, of which **134** met the >=90 minutes/under-200-words flag. This is a partial assembly; additional files (v84, v85, v87, v91) have been authored and were not included in that screening total.

## Academic caveats and next work
A 200-word heuristic is a **deficiency screen, not proof** of MBA-level teaching or actual allocated study hours. An instructional lesson assigned 105–180 minutes should be paired with independently solved exercises, reading, writing, model critique and time estimates supported by real pilot learners. The new 14-lesson expansions are strong initial remediation, but do not close the wider Level 3 gap.
