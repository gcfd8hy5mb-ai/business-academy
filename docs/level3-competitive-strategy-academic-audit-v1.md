## Academic level and gaps

**Strengths:** Strategic choices are translated into contribution economics; multiple downside scenarios and governance prompts; cross-functional integration of pricing, acquisition, entry and platform strategy; some game-theory interpretation.

**High-priority deficiencies:**
1. Too much of the assessed work is stylized, fictional and has supplied calculations. Demonstrated performance with messy, independently sourced real corporate information is not established.
2. No verified five-forces, resource-based view, activity-system or value-chain analysis across a documented actual competitive set and defined market boundary.
3. Rival response assumptions lack explicit estimation methods, rival-specific evidence, market concentration, substitutes and regulatory constraints.
4. Strategy implementation needs organization design, talent/capabilities, sequencing, competitive intelligence ethics and falsifiable milestones.
5. The app's self-rated written rubrics are not independently graded. Automated correctness checks are not evidence of graduate-level synthesis.
6. No blind unseen strategy cases, formal quality moderation or calibrated live debate demonstrated.

## Proposed assessed graduate strategy case (new assessment design; not yet implemented)

**Executive brief:** Study a public company's competitive advantage through at least three years of primary annual reports and current segment disclosures. Define strategic groups, customers, rivals and relevant geography; distinguish financial metrics from management claims. Choose between defend/expand/exit decisions. Use primary documents and only publicly lawful sources. Require a plausible alternative and adversarial board response.

**Required analysis:** industry forces and their changes; activity-system tradeoffs; quantitative unit economics and price/volume/cannibalization sensitivities; rivals' incentives and best responses; resource constraints; antitrust and governance concerns; scenario-adjusted value; leading indicators and decision reversal triggers.

**Review evidence:** three original written artifacts per learner including an initial strategy memo, falsification memo with counterevidence, and revised final board recommendation; independently graded blind against **analysis 25%, financial logic 20%, evidence 20%, alternatives/implementation 20%, communication 15%**. Proposed project threshold: 80/100 overall and no critical arithmetic or sourcing errors. Proposed percentages are an internal quality standard, not a university or accreditor's threshold.
