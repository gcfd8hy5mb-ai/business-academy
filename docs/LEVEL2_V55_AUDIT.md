## Three integrated projects and final

1. **Peregrine multinational performance review** (hosted in course 08): reconcile consolidated segment results, CFO and capital expenditure; compare full and phased automation after tax, liquidity and workforce constraints.
2. **Astra Pacific market expansion** (course 10): translate a randomized conversion lift into constrained cohort economics, test a warehouse expansion and compare pricing under competitor response.
3. **Helix Global restructuring and risk** (course 12): reconcile a cash floor with debt extension, compare full versus staged restructuring, and identify contract, employment and governance controls.

Each simulation includes a 90-minute teaching/transfer brief, a 120-minute independent model, a 120-minute five-question plus written exam, and a 300-minute board portfolio. The **Level 2 cumulative final** is hosted as additional course 13 evidence: a 120-minute synthesis brief, a 240-minute 14-question examination with a separate 1,800–2,200 word board paper, and a 360-minute seven-discipline capstone evidence pack. Its 80% objective gate and written rubric review are distinct; the app does not claim secure proctoring or independent essay grading. Completion of course 13 alone is not a verified graduation decision across all seven courses.
