Level 1 now contains **36 authored lessons, 36 original objective practice questions, 12 new multi-step written application problems, 30 original objective examination questions, 24 new advanced exam questions, 12 written examination cases and 12 portfolio projects/evidence packs**. Its assigned workload is **111.50 estimated hours** against the original **365-hour plan**. These are study estimates for actual prompts, reading and written work, not time spent by a student or awarded credit. The remaining 253.50 hours in the Level 1 design target are not delivered content.

The incremental 10.25 hours per subject allocates 105 minutes to three new lessons with worked and transfer exercises, 120 minutes to two written problems, 120 minutes to a four-question and written case exam, 180 minutes to an evidence pack and 90 minutes to a specific external source assignment. Time estimates are approximate; complexity and prior knowledge vary. The four objective questions are only one component of a longer exam, not a stand-alone university examination.

New instruction covers invested capital and governance, annuities and value of information, reconciled profit bridges and capacity, venture options and security preferences, strategic pricing and macro scenarios, and cash-flow/covenant analysis. Cases use fictional companies and explicitly stated assumptions. Board packs require dated corporate evidence, calculation trails, counterarguments, decision rights and a monitorable recommendation. Model responses and analytic self-review rubrics support revision; there is no independent essay grading or live AI professor.

## Evidence and preservation

The original Level 1 assessments, draft keys, answers, attempts, portfolio files and first 18 lesson IDs are unchanged. New modules use `m4`–`m6` and new assessment IDs. Previously submitted scores and essays remain visible. Expanded sequence mastery adds completion of the new lessons, both new written applications, at least 75% on the additional objective case exam, a separately self-reviewed advanced written case, and a separately self-reviewed executive evidence pack. An earlier self-assessed mastery label is reconsidered against these additional academic requirements; the earlier evidence remains stored. Editing an already reviewed draft clears its old rubric ratings until reviewed again.

Guided professor responses reveal one authored step at a time, save the learner’s explanation and preserve step position. Incorrect submitted answers show the full calculation or reasoning, a remediation prompt and a link to the related lesson. This is local, deterministic teaching; it cannot judge a student’s prose.
