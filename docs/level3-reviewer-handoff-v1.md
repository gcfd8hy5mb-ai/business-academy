## Required review sequence
1. Assign two qualified independent reviewers and document each one's qualification and conflicts.
2. Supply unseen/new work, learner-authored models, exact primary citations and an anonymized student code.
3. Independently recompute every material model conclusion and inspect documentary evidence.
4. Score technical accuracy (30), decision reasoning (25), research (20), governance (15), and communication (10).
5. Log critical mistakes. Hold a new-question defense or equivalent supervised assessment.
6. Reconcile reviewers' marks, request remediation, and record the final dated decision.
